## Current causal diagnosis

The v1 deployed result conflated two components. With its original gate, flow-1,000 best-generated error was 6.03067° and generated candidates beat the 6.02714° base on only 3.91% of clips. Applying those exact sampled residuals without the learned gate changes the result to 5.63091° and 82.03%. Therefore the evidence rejects “no generator headroom”; it supports “v1 Q/gate suppresses useful residuals.”

The redesign is intentionally versioned rather than patched into v1: v2 fits residual coordinates from training data only, makes the Q label materially less imbalanced (3° body / 5° hand), reports discrimination as well as calibration, and fits gate thresholds on one development fold before auditing them on another.

The v2 Q release run completed all 2,500 declared steps. Its selected checkpoint is step 2,500, SHA-256 `704c5dcc00b30c3e77ba427d1573f455e1624b5dcd167cb459a157b9e8ddae4e`, with positive prevalence 0.67939, AUROC 0.98642, average precision 0.99439, balanced accuracy 0.95202, Brier 0.04074, ECE-15 0.03001 and log loss 0.14185 on the declared mixture-validation protocol. The separate uncorrupted natural audit also passed: overall/body/hands AUROC 0.97221/0.90734/0.96918 and probability–error Pearson −0.88401/−0.79494/−0.90810. Natural overall prevalence is 0.46407, Brier 0.05522 and ECE-15 0.02978.

The full v2 deterministic A3 control completed at step 10,000 and selected that final checkpoint (SHA-256 `31b9644824501d0eb504c664ac8fd7164cbcacf400cf68c292c728128313ca95`; validation residual MSE 0.21981). On the locked 128-clip development manifest, gated overall/body/hands error is 1.9018/1.3027/2.1414° versus base 6.0271/2.5456/7.4198°, with improvement on 128/128 clips. Across 52 source groups, the 10,000-replicate paired cluster-bootstrap delta is −4.1253° (95% CI [−4.2113, −4.0380], improvement probability 1.0). Removing Q/gating yields 1.7788° overall and delta −4.2483° [−4.3368, −4.1569], so Q still suppresses some useful correction but no longer destroys A3 headroom. These numbers use source-disjoint How2Sign pseudo-targets and the SMPLer-X-only initializer; they do not establish mocap accuracy or the required strong-A1 result.

The v2 normalized flow completed the same 10,000-step budget with final validation flow MSE 0.83194. Its append-only Q-bound checkpoint is SHA-256 `44aef8ad6c1afc195f3f6c3f0d49a6208dd7916d08b5715e413cf7ebff6da9f5`; all 83 model tensors equal the source checkpoint bit-for-bit and the locked loader verifies Q/statistics/config bindings. Calibrated K4 development headroom is 2.3032° at K1 and 2.2368° for A6 oracle. Targetless geometry selection obtains 2.2997° with a 0.0629° oracle regret, so the generator passes coverage but the current selector adds little over K1/random. This result is carried forward as a limitation rather than a positive form/selection claim.

The v1 SGNify pilot is retained as an executed artifact but is not a clean A7 implementation after the refinement-scope audit. Across its saved targetless inference artifacts, out-of-scope joints had mean gate 0.62801 and a 66.11% nonzero rate, compared with mean 0.07248 and 7.94% nonzero on supported joints. Root/spine rotations can move large mesh regions even when local geodesic diagnostics exclude them, so this deviation is a plausible contributor to the reported body degradation. It cannot be causally quantified by tuning on the already exposed SGNify test set.

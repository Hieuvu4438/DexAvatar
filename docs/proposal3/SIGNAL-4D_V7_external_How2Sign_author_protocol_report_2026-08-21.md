## Executive conclusion

V7 external How2Sign residual transport is a protocol-valid, zero-SGNify-
training candidate. It was frozen before official evaluation, produced all 57
signs and 1,493 frames, and was evaluated with the repository's unmodified
adapter to the released author code.

Relative to V6, V7 reduces author-protocol TR-V2V UBody-F by **0.1514 mm
(0.513%)** and UBody by **0.1555 mm (0.595%)**. Paired sign-level bootstrap
intervals exclude zero for both regions. The All score regresses by **0.2653
mm (0.630%)** because the transported rotations alter vertices outside the
upper-body evaluation mask through the coherent SMPL-X kinematic surface.
Hands are practically unchanged, with a tiny measurable RHand regression.

The defensible paper statement is:

> With parameters trained and selected only on external How2Sign data, the
> proposed residual-transport module improves SIGNAL-4D V6 UBody-F from
> 29.519 to 29.368 mm and UBody from 26.139 to 25.984 mm under the released
> SGNify author protocol at full 1,493-frame coverage.

Do not replace that sentence with “V7 improves all metrics”, “V7 is full-body
SOTA”, or “the result comes from a pristine unseen benchmark”.

## What V7 does

The external T2 checkpoint predicts bounded local SO(3) corrections from
image-derived whole-body confidence, presence, temporal motion, and 2D
reprojection residuals. It does not replace SMPLer-X/DexAvatar and it does not
use SGNify target meshes.

For each locked upper-body joint, V7 computes the checkpoint correction
relative to its own initializer and transports that correction onto V6:

`DeltaR = R_external R_initializer^T`

`R_V7 = DeltaR R_V6`

The transported joints are spine1/2/3, neck, left/right collar,
left/right shoulder, and left/right elbow. Root, lower body, jaw, eyes, hand
poses, shape, expression, and translation come from V6. Local wrist rotations
are compensated after changing their ancestor chain so both global wrist
orientations remain equal to V6. Each output is generated by one coherent
SMPL-X forward pass; vertices are never spliced between methods.

The residual scale is fixed at `alpha=1.0`. No SGNify search over alpha,
joints, thresholds, checkpoint, or frame routing was performed after the
official evaluation.

## Paired sign-level uncertainty

The secondary analysis resamples the 57 sign clips as paired units with
100,000 percentile-bootstrap replicates and seed 12345. Its estimand is the
equal-weight sign-macro mean of `V7 - V6`, which differs slightly from the
official vertex-micro primary score.

| Region | Eligible signs | Mean delta (mm) | 95% bootstrap CI (mm) | Improved / worse signs |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| All | 57 | +0.289281 | [ +0.224117, +0.353340 ] | 5 / 52 |
| UBody | 57 | -0.131790 | [ -0.228553, -0.033603 ] | 36 / 21 |
| UBody-F | 57 | -0.126628 | [ -0.221707, -0.030069 ] | 34 / 23 |
| LHand | 42 | +0.000024 | [ -0.000534, +0.000582 ] | 21 / 21 |
| RHand | 57 | +0.002853 | [ +0.002179, +0.003540 ] | 9 / 48 |

The UBody and UBody-F improvements are consistent under vertex-micro and
sign-macro aggregation. They are heterogeneous across signs and small in
absolute magnitude. The left-hand interval contains zero. The right-hand
change is consistently positive but only 0.0029 mm sign-macro and 0.024%
relative in the official aggregation. The left-hand author evaluation excludes
class-0 clips by the released code, leaving 42 eligible clips.

No formal family-wise multiplicity correction is claimed. UBody-F was the
preregistered primary endpoint; UBody, All, and hands are supporting safety
endpoints. The reported bootstrap tail probabilities are descriptive and are
not relabeled as exact parametric p-values.

## Fallacy and validity scan

1. **Simpson's paradox — NOTE.** Official vertex-micro and secondary
   sign-macro estimates agree in direction for UBody and UBody-F. Per-sign
   heterogeneity remains visible and is reported.
2. **Ecological fallacy — NOTE.** Claims are limited to benchmark signs and
   frames; no signer-population or individual-level claim is made.
3. **Berkson's bias — CAUTION.** The 57 available SGNify signs are a selected
   benchmark sample. External validity requires a second sealed dataset.
4. **Collider bias — NOTE.** No post-treatment covariate adjustment is used.
5. **Base-rate neglect — NOTE.** This is not a classification or prevalence
   study.
6. **Regression to the mean — NOTE.** All manifest frames are evaluated; no
   extreme-error subset was selected for the final comparison.
7. **Survivorship bias — NOTE.** Coverage is 100%; failed or difficult frames
   were not dropped.
8. **Look-elsewhere effect — CAUTION.** Five regions are reported, and older
   method iterations inspected this benchmark. UBody-F was locked as primary,
   but no global multiple-testing claim is made.
9. **Garden of forking paths — CAUTION.** This integration was preregistered
   before its official run, but it follows earlier SIGNAL-4D benchmark
   exploration. That history prevents a pristine confirmatory label.
10. **Correlation versus causation — CAUTION.** The paired deterministic
    intervention identifies this code change on this benchmark; it does not
    establish universal causal benefit across datasets or capture conditions.
11. **Reverse causality — NOTE.** It is not applicable to this controlled
    algorithmic comparison.

## Claim policy for the paper

Allowed now:

- an external-only, zero-SGNify-training V7 upper-body result;
- exact UBody and UBody-F improvements over frozen V6 under the released
  author protocol;
- full 1,493-frame coverage, preserved hand articulation contract, and exact
  reproducibility in the current environment.

Not allowed yet:

- global literature SOTA without a like-for-like table against published
  methods and protocols;
- full-body superiority, because All regresses;
- a pristine held-out-test claim, because SGNify was inspected during prior
  V5–V7 development;
- improvement to hand reconstruction, because LHand is neutral and RHand
  slightly regresses.

For a stronger main-paper claim, freeze this exact implementation and evaluate
it once on an additional sealed signer- or dataset-level test set. Report V6
and V7 together with paired sign-level intervals and retain every frame.
